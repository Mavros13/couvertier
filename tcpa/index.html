TCPA consent

Effective November 5, 2026 · Couvertier Premier Advisory

Your consent

When you check the consent box on the quote form and submit it, you agree that Couvertier Premier Advisory and its licensed advisors may contact you about your quote at the phone number and email address you provided. Contact may be by phone, text, or email, and calls or texts may use automated technology.

If you leave the form without finishing

If you enter your name and phone number and leave without finishing, what you entered may be saved so an advisor can follow up. Saving it does not by itself mean you agreed to marketing calls or texts. That consent is given by checking the box and submitting the form.

If you agree to be contacted

Couvertier Premier Advisory and its licensed advisors may then contact you about health insurance options at the phone number and email address you provided. Contact may be by phone, text, or email, and calls or texts may use automated technology.

Consent is not a condition of a quote

Agreeing to be contacted is not required to get a quote or to buy a product. You can call (954) 892-0550.

Message frequency and rates

Message frequency depends on the request. Message and data rates may apply. Wireless carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

How to opt out

  • Texts: reply STOP to stop texts. Reply HELP for help.
  • Calls and email: say so on a call, call (954) 892-0550, or email couvertier@couvertierpremier.com.
  • You may withdraw consent at any time. You can also register a number at donotcall.gov.

Your phone number

If you give a phone number, you confirm that you are its subscriber or customary user and that you may give this consent.
